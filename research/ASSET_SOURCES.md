# Asset sources

Product photography is downloaded and bundled in `public/products/`, never fetched by the deployed storefront from a product API. Seed metadata was consulted once via DummyJSON; prices, ratings, stock, descriptions, and recommendations in this demo are illustrative.

- Electronics, home, fashion, and sports: https://cdn.dummyjson.com/product-images/ (source URLs and product selection in `scripts/seed-catalog.cjs`). Brands remain accurately named where present.
- Controller photo: https://unsplash.com/photos/1600080972464-8e5f35f63d08 (image endpoint recorded in the seed script).
- Keyboard photo: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1587829741301-dc798b83add3 (image endpoint).
- Fictional book covers: original repository-native SVG artwork.
- Icons: Lucide.
- Amazon research screenshots are references only. No Amazon logo or product page assets are used in the storefront.

This is a non-commercial assignment demo; no affiliation with Amazon or the depicted manufacturers is claimed.
